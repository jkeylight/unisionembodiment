Who we are

UNISON EMBODIMENT is a founder-led embodiment practice run by Poonam Mahendra, based in Mumbai, India. For the purposes of this policy, Poonam Mahendra is the data fiduciary responsible for the personal information described here.

This policy applies to information you share through this website, by email, by telephone, by WhatsApp, or in the course of a session. It does not apply to third-party websites we may link to.

What we collect

We collect only what is needed to respond to you and to hold a session safely. That is usually:

  • Your name
  • An email address or telephone number
  • Your time zone or city, so we can agree a time
  • Whatever you choose to tell us about what you are seeking
  • Session notes, if you become a client — see below

We do not collect payment card details directly. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

Why we collect it

We use your information to:

  • Reply to your enquiry and answer questions about the work
  • Arrange, reschedule or cancel a session
  • Prepare for a session and keep a factual record afterwards
  • Meet legal, tax and safeguarding obligations

Our lawful basis is your consent when you contact us, and the practical steps needed to provide a service you have asked for. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to us.

Session notes and confidentiality

After a session, brief factual notes may be kept: what you said you wanted to explore, the themes that arose, what practices were offered, your reported experience, consent given, and any agreed next step.

These notes exist for continuity of care and for professional accountability. They are not shared, sold or used for marketing. They are not shown to anyone else unless:

  • You ask us to, in writing
  • We are legally required to
  • There is a serious and imminent risk of harm to you or to someone else
  • We believe a child or a vulnerable adult is at risk

In those limited cases, only what is necessary would be disclosed, and where it is safe and lawful to do so, we would tell you first.

Sharing

We do not share personal information with anyone except:

  • A professional supervisor, where case material is discussed anonymously or with identifying details removed
  • A payment provider, if you pay electronically — they receive only what the transaction requires
  • A legal or safeguarding authority, in the limited circumstances described above

Information may be stored or processed on servers outside India, depending on the tools in use. Where that happens, it is protected by the contractual terms those providers offer.

Storage and security

Enquiries are kept in a password-protected email account. Session notes are stored separately from contact details where the tools allow, on devices protected by a passcode. We do not use shared or public computers to store client information.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach that could affect you, we will tell you what happened, what information was involved, and what we are doing about it.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask for it to be corrected if it is wrong or out of date
  • Ask for it to be erased, unless we must keep it by law
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Ask us to stop using your information for a particular purpose
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf
  • Raise a grievance, and approach the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied with our response

To exercise any of these, write to info@unisonembodiment.com. We aim to respond within seven working days.

Cookies and third parties

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log a standard server request including your IP address. If you prefer, you can block fonts in your browser; the site will fall back to system fonts.

Links to Instagram or other sites take you to third parties with their own privacy practices. We are not responsible for those.

Children

Sessions are offered to adults only. This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, the date at the top of the page changes. If a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will contact you directly rather than rely on a website update.
