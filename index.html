The practice

An intuitive, awareness-led
and integrative practice.

UNISON is an intuitive, awareness-led and integrative practice founded by Poonam Mahendra.

Drawing upon years of personal exploration, learning, spiritual practice and experiential work, Poonam brings together intuitive perception, energetic awareness, ancestral and inner-child exploration, somatic awareness and spiritual integration.

Her approach is guided by the understanding that each individual has their own experience, inner resources and capacity for self-discovery.

Through attentive presence, intuitive exploration, reflective dialogue and dedicated energy-work practices, clients are offered a space to explore their experiences, recognise patterns and develop a deeper awareness of themselves.

The work may incorporate chakra-based energy practices, subtle-body awareness and spiritual frameworks, according to the nature of the session and the practitioner’s training and scope.

The cycle

Nothing to fix.
Everything to notice.

01

Recognise

See the pattern as it is happening, not only in hindsight. What recurs, and where it lives.

02

Accept

Let it be true without arguing with it. Acceptance is not agreement — it is where movement starts.

03

Interrupt

Choose differently at the moment the pattern usually runs. A small pause is enough.

04

Integrate

Let the new choice settle into the body, so it is available without effort.

05

Evolve

The history stays. What it determines does not have to.

Five dimensions

Not compartments.
Lenses.

The map is useful because it slows down premature conclusions. Instead of asking only why you are like this, you can ask what is actually here.

01

Body

Sensation, posture, breath as it actually is. Comfort, discomfort, and the physical boundaries you carry.

02

Mind

Thoughts, beliefs, interpretations, conditioning. The old learning that plays again in new rooms.

03

Emotion

What you feel, what you need, what you learned to do with both. Every emotion deserves recognition. Every action remains your choice.

04

Soul / Meaning

Values, purpose, devotion, connection — the meanings you choose, not the ones handed to you.

05

Awareness

The capacity to notice experience without immediately becoming it. Not a state to attain. Something already here.
