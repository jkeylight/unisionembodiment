01 — The approach

Not a method to master. A space to listen.

UNISON is an intuitive, awareness-led and integrative practice founded by Poonam Mahendra.

PresenceChoiceIntegrationNothing is forced

The premise

Wholeness is not manufactured.

Drawing upon years of personal exploration, learning, spiritual practice and experiential work, Poonam brings together intuitive perception, energetic awareness, ancestral and inner-child exploration, somatic awareness and spiritual integration.

Her approach is guided by the understanding that each individual has their own experience, inner resources and capacity for self-discovery.

Through attentive presence, intuitive exploration, reflective dialogue and dedicated energy-work practices, clients are offered a space to explore their experiences, recognise patterns and develop a deeper awareness of themselves.

The map

Five dimensions, held together by awareness.

They are lenses for inquiry — not separate compartments, and not a diagnostic model. A physical sensation may have a medical explanation. A feeling may be a response to something happening now.

01

Body

Sensation, movement, posture, comfort, discomfort, breath as it naturally is. The body is where much of what we carry actually lives — in tightness, numbness, heat, weight.

02

Mind

Thoughts, beliefs, conditioning, identity, the habitual narratives that shape how a situation is read. Much of what we call thinking is remembering — old learning replayed in new circumstances.

03

Emotion

Vocabulary, needs, expression, resilience, response. Emotions are information about what matters — not problems to be solved, and not instructions to be followed without question.

04

Soul / Meaning

Values, purpose, devotion, connection — the meanings a person chooses to explore. Held without imposing any belief. For some this is prayer; for others, nature, silence, service or love.

05

Awareness

The capacity to notice experience without immediately being defined by it. Not a state to achieve. Something that is already operating, and can be recognised.

The cycle

Recognise. Accept. Interrupt.
Integrate. Evolve.

The work may incorporate chakra-based energy practices, subtle-body awareness and spiritual frameworks, according to the nature of the session and the practitioner’s training and scope. These describe what a person may notice. They are not steps to complete, and nobody moves through them in order.

  1. 01

    Recognise

    Patterns and recurring experiences, seen as they are happening rather than in hindsight.

  2. 02

    Accept

    Letting it be true without arguing with it. Acceptance is not agreement — it is where movement begins.

  3. 03

    Interrupt

    Choosing differently at the point the pattern usually runs. A small pause is enough.

  4. 04

    Integrate

    Allowing the new choice to settle, so it is available without effort.

  5. 05

    Evolve

    The history stays. What it determines does not have to.

The boundaries

What this is, and what it is not.

It is

  • A respectful process
  • Clear boundaries and informed choice
  • Honest communication about scope
  • Chakra-based and spiritual frameworks, held lightly
  • Work that follows your pace
  • Complementary to medical care

It is not

  • A promise of cure
  • Diagnosis or treatment
  • Guaranteed emotional release
  • Recovered memories or revelation
  • A fixed timeline or result
Care note

This is complementary care. It does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment, and it does not claim to cure any condition. If something is acute or changing quickly, please see your doctor first. This work can sit alongside that care.
